About

Ros Veluz

Graphic, web and UI/UX designer based in the Philippines.

I help businesses and organizations communicate clearly through websites, publications, brand identities, and digital interfaces.

Over more than a decade of design work, I've moved between print and digital: laying out publications, developing visual identities, designing websites, and working with product and development teams on user interfaces.

What connects that work is an interest in the details that help people understand what is in front of them. Whether it's a product, a service, or an organization's story, I want the design to make the important information easier to find and the next step easier to take.

Working together

I value a clear brief, regular progress updates, and honest conversations about what a project needs. I ask questions early, flag gaps in content or requirements, and seek feedback along the way so everyone knows where the work stands.

My role often extends beyond the layout itself: working with developers, checking interfaces, preparing files for production, and helping the team resolve the details needed to complete a project.

I'm particularly interested in communications work for established businesses, trade unions, and organizations. Considered design can give complex information a clear structure without competing with the message.

Beyond the screen

I draw, sketch, and make things by hand. I'm often thinking about what to cook next, especially something grilled or a good sandwich. Music is another long-running interest, from exploring theory to looking for wood for a musical instrument project.

I still have an ambitious creative streak, including the old dream of making something worthy of a museum wall. For now, I'm glad to put that curiosity to work on useful, thoughtful design.
