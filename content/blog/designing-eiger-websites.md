## Related Companies, Different Priorities

My website design work for Eiger Operating and Eiger Resources gave each company a distinct visual identity within a shared brand. The designs brought together company information, owner support, growth milestones, and leadership profiles.

This article looks at the design work shown in my portfolio. It is about presentation and information structure, rather than a claim about traffic, enquiries, or other measured results.

Related businesses do not necessarily need identical websites. They need enough continuity to make their relationship understandable, while allowing each site to give its own audience the appropriate information.

For these designs, that distinction appears in the content as well as the visual treatment. Eiger Operating includes owner relations and support information. Eiger Resources presents company background, operating principles, growth, and leadership.

## Giving Support Information a Clear Place

A corporate homepage is only one part of a website. The supporting pages matter just as much when someone arrives with a specific question.

The Eiger Operating owner relations design gives support information and frequently asked questions their own space. That is a different communication task from introducing the company: visitors need to identify the relevant topic and find a useful next step.

![Eiger Operating owner relations page design](/img/projects/eiger-websites/eigerWebsite-ownerRelations-desktop.webp "wide: Owner relations information and FAQs within the Eiger Operating website design.")

The broader design principle is to separate general introductions from practical information without making the two feel disconnected. A recognizable page structure lets supporting content belong to the same website while giving it room to do a different job.

## Making Company History Scannable

The Eiger Resources growth section presents milestones as a sequence. On desktop, the timeline runs horizontally; the mobile design changes it to a vertical sequence.

That change preserves the relationship between dates and events while adapting the reading order to a narrower screen. Responsive design is not only about shrinking a composition. Sometimes the content needs a different arrangement.

![Eiger Resources company growth timeline](/img/projects/eiger-websites/eigerResources-growth-desktop.webp "full: A dated sequence gives the growth section an organized reading order.")

A timeline also gives a long company story a clear structure. Visitors can first identify the major events, then read the supporting details. The design provides an overview without replacing the explanation.

## Leadership as Part of the Communication

The leadership designs use consistent portrait and biography treatments. The individual profiles remain distinct, but their presentation follows a common pattern.

This is the kind of detail I value in corporate work. A page should make the information easy to compare and read without turning every profile into a separate visual exercise.

The same thinking applies across company overviews, news, and contact sections: each type of content needs an appropriate hierarchy within the shared design.

## What I Take from This Work

The Eiger designs reflect an approach I want to bring to more business and organizational websites: understand the different communication tasks, give each a clear place, and maintain continuity across the whole experience.

A considered corporate website can have character without asking its visitors to work around the design. The content, navigation, and responsive layouts should support the reason someone came to the site.
