Ways to work together

Phase-based work

Bring me in for a specific stage, or work through the full process together. We agree on the scope and review points for each phase before it begins.

  1. Concept developmentExplore the brief, develop directions and agree on the visual approach.
  2. Production workBuild out the approved direction across the required pages, layouts or assets.
  3. Art finalizationRefine the details, check specifications and prepare the final files for print, digital delivery or development handoff.

Retainer arrangement

Ongoing design support for teams with recurring needs. We agree on a regular allocation of time or deliverables, then plan priorities and turnaround around that capacity.

A good fit for recurring communications, marketing materials and design updates.

Project-based work

A defined engagement for a specific outcome, with an agreed brief, deliverables, schedule and fee. We set feedback milestones and discuss any changes to scope as the work progresses.

A good fit for a website design, visual identity, publication or campaign.

Discuss your project

Tell me what you need, your timeline and whether the work is a one-off project or ongoing. We can work out the right scope and arrangement together.
